# DA, DR, Authority Score and link decisions

Use for every article plan, competitor comparison and backlink audit. Consider all three requested indicators when data is available; lack of a paid account is a documented limitation, not a reason to invent values or stop the whole audit.

| Indicator | Vendor and purpose | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Domain Authority (DA) | Moz; proprietary domain-level comparative search-strength estimate | Compare relevant domains within Moz's methodology and collection date; not Google's own score |
| Domain Rating (DR) | Ahrefs; relative strength of a domain's backlink profile, logarithmic 0–100 scale | Distinct from page-level URL Rating; change may reflect the wider comparison set, not a site change alone |
| Authority Score | Semrush; compound indicator incorporating link power, estimated organic traffic and natural-profile/spam signals | Investigate the components; a high aggregate does not certify editorial quality or safety |

Definitions: [Moz authority-scoring guide](https://moz-static.s3.amazonaws.com/products/landing-pages/announcements/Authority_Scoring_Guide.pdf), [Ahrefs DR](https://ahrefs.com/seo/glossary/domain-rating), [Semrush Authority Score](https://www.semrush.com/kb/747-authority-score-backlink-scores). These are proprietary third-party measures with different datasets/methods. Do not average, convert or equate DA 50, DR 50 and Authority Score 50. They are not official Google ranking factors, guarantees, link prices or universal accept/reject thresholds.

## Collection contract

For own domain, relevant competitor domains, prospective referring domains and important existing referring domains record: exact target; domain/subdomain scope; DA, DR and Authority Score individually; provider; collection date/time; source report/API or supplied export; database/country where applicable to traffic metrics; available page-level context; limitations. Missing means **unavailable**, not zero. A user-supplied export keeps its original date and provenance; never label it live.

Use authenticated tools, authorized APIs, official free data or supplied exports. Do not bypass access limits or buy a subscription without authority. Corroborate substantial findings with actual source pages and site behavior. Differences between providers are expected; document them rather than “correcting” one to match another.

## Article decisions

Capture the actual local SERP for the topic. Compare ranking pages' intent fit, specificity, evidence, usefulness, freshness, format and relevant page links, alongside the three domain metrics. A strong domain can have a weak page; a specialist low-score domain may be highly relevant. Use the comparison to choose a credible angle, valuable evidence and achievable content scope, not to promise easy ranking or forbid valuable evergreen content.

Choose article citations primarily for factual authority and primary evidence. A university, regulator or small specialist should not be discarded solely for unavailable/low vendor scores. Domain metrics do not prove a source's claims. Internal linking connects relevant pages naturally; do not force exact-match anchors to chase score changes.

## Backlink decisions

Inspect source page/domain, real editorial ownership, topical connection, audience language/market, relevance of target page and anchor, visible placement, link attributes, indexability/access, apparent audience and traffic provenance, outbound-link patterns and obvious manipulation. Add DA/DR/Authority Score as supporting evidence. Distinguish observed traffic from vendor estimates.

Classify: credible earned opportunity; relevant citation/partnership needing independent outreach authorization; existing link to repair/reclaim; needs investigation; reject for manipulation/unrelated placement. High scores never override evidence of a link farm, purchased ranking placement or hacked pages. No quotas, automated acquisition, fake mentions, reciprocal schemes or guaranteed “dofollow” authority transfer.

Do not send outreach merely because a prospect list exists. Do not remove/disavow links based only on a toxicity flag or low score; investigate actual policy risk and obtain authority for consequential changes. Paid/sponsored and UGC placements need appropriate disclosure/attributes under current platform guidance.
